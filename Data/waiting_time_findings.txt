CareFlow Project – Week 2 Findings

Objective

Analyze patient waiting times between consecutive healthcare activities and identify potential workflow bottlenecks.

Results

- Longest average transition: Lab Test → Discharge — 120 minutes.
- Second-longest average: Triage → Doctor Consultation — 46.67 minutes.
- Third-longest average: Doctor Consultation → Discharge — 45 minutes.
- Registration → Triage averages 25 minutes.
- Doctor Consultation → Lab Test averages 22.5 minutes.

Interpretation

The Lab Test → Discharge transition has the highest observed average time. This may indicate a delay between laboratory testing and patient discharge. The Triage → Doctor Consultation transition also warrants investigation.

Limitations

The results cover a small number of cases. The Lab Test → Discharge transition includes only two cases, so additional data is needed before concluding that this is a consistent bottleneck.

Next Steps

1. Validate the timestamps and waiting-time calculations.
2. Investigate the longest transitions.
3. Collect more patient-case data.
4. Compare waiting times across cases and activities.
5. Integrate the findings into the CareFlow dashboard.
